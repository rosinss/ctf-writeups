## 01 / Competition Overview

iHack CTF 2026 was a cybersecurity competition consisting of a Jeopardy-style qualification round and an Attack & Defense final round.

This directory documents my competition experience, technical investigations, challenge solutions and lessons learned as a member of Team Dot RaR.

| Final Round | 17 November 2026 |
| Achievement | Attack & Defense Finalist |
