## 02 / Competition Objectives

- Identify vulnerabilities in provided services.
- Exploit vulnerabilities within authorised targets.
- Protect team services from exploitation.
- Maintain service availability.
- Recover and submit competition flags.

## 03 / Competition Preparation

Before the competition, I developed a personal cybersecurity home lab using Kali Linux, Docker, Python and Flask.

The home lab focused on:

- Broken access control (IDOR)
- Authentication and authorisation
- SQL injection
- Path traversal
- Vulnerability assessment
- Service hardening
- Log monitoring
- Incident response and service recovery

[View Attack & Defense CTF Home Lab](https://github.com/rosinss/attack-defense-ctf-homelab)

## 04 / Competition Experience

The final round introduced a more challenging environment than traditional Jeopardy-style CTF competitions.

The competition required participants to work under time constraints while following strict rules governing service exploitation and infrastructure access.

One difficulty encountered involved handling HTTP redirections when interacting with competition services.

## 05 / Lessons Learned

- Importance of understanding application source code.
- Importance of maintaining service availability.
- Effective communication and teamwork.
- Troubleshooting under time pressure.
- Importance of practical cybersecurity training.
- Balancing offensive and defensive security.

## 06 / Future Improvements
